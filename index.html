FAQ

Common
questions

Can't find what you're looking for? We're happy to jump on a quick call.

Still unsure? Send us a message or book a free discovery call — no commitment required.
How long does a typical project take?
Most Starter projects are delivered in 3–5 business days. Growth projects typically take 1–2 weeks depending on complexity. We'll give you a firm timeline after the discovery call — we don't start until you're comfortable with the scope.
What tools and platforms do you support?
We work with virtually any tool that has an API or webhook — including Make, n8n, Zapier, HubSpot, Salesforce, Shopify, Stripe, Airtable, Notion, Slack, Google Workspace, and many more. If it has an API, we can integrate it. For custom or internal tools, we write the code directly.
Do I need technical knowledge to work with you?
Not at all. We work with founders, ops leads, and teams who just know they're doing too much manually. You describe the problem in plain English — we handle everything technical. We'll keep you updated throughout in non-technical language.
What happens if something breaks after launch?
Every project includes a post-launch support window (14 days for Starter, 30 days for Growth). During this time, any bug related to our build is fixed free of charge. Retainer clients get ongoing priority support indefinitely. We also set up monitoring so issues are caught before you notice them.
Can you work with our existing automations?
Yes — we audit, fix, extend, and migrate existing automations all the time. We'll start by reviewing what you have and give you an honest assessment of what's worth keeping, what needs refactoring, and what should be rebuilt from scratch.
